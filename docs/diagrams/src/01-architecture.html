Three guarantees qedra makes

Formal proofs for Azure architecture, not heuristics.
Security

Prove the data tier cannot be reached from the public internet. Z3 returns the exact packet when it can.

Reliability

Prove composite availability meets its target, computed from a continuous-time Markov chain.

Proof

Every verdict ships a machine-checkable certificate: a minimal UNSAT core, or a re-validated counterexample.
